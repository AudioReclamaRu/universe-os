# Canonical research principles — UNIVERSE / GIZA / RSE

## Canonical motto

> **Отсутствие доказательства не является доказательством отсутствия.**

This is a permanent governing principle for research across UNIVERSE, GIZA PRIME, RSE, the Great Comma and all connected investigations.

It must be consulted whenever the next research step is unclear, whenever a candidate appears unsupported, whenever an object or mechanism is missing from the current model, and whenever an investigation risks being closed only because evidence has not yet been found.

## Operational meaning

The motto means:

1. **Do not equate non-detection with non-existence.** An unobserved object, feature, field, process or relation remains unresolved unless the investigation had a realistic opportunity to detect it.
2. **Do not reverse the error.** Lack of disproof is not proof of existence. An open possibility is not an established fact.
3. **Investigate why evidence is absent.** Examine preservation, erosion, later modification, burial, access, sampling coverage, measurement resolution, instrument sensitivity, noise, assumptions and the suitability of the method.
4. **Model the maximum plausible picture before narrowing it.** Preserve alternative objects, original forms, mechanisms and spatial configurations as explicit candidate branches. Do not discard a candidate merely because its current form is damaged, eroded, hidden or not yet measured.
5. **Use idealization deliberately.** Where measurements are missing, define one or more idealized candidates from stated geometrical, mathematical or physical principles. Assign assumptions, parameter ranges, constraints and confidence labels. Do not silently substitute ideal values for observations.
6. **Keep evidence layers distinct.** Mark every parameter as measured, source-reported, reconstructed, idealized, inferred, assumed or unknown. An ideal is a research object, not a historical fact.
7. **Search for a decisive test.** For each open candidate, specify what observation would support it, weaken it, distinguish it from alternatives or falsify it.

## Required response when the next step is unclear

Return to this motto and ask, in order:

- What possibility have we prematurely excluded only because it has not been demonstrated?
- What could have hidden, destroyed, transformed or made the evidence difficult to detect?
- Which idealized forms or mechanisms can be built without pretending they were measured?
- What independent data or test could discriminate between the candidate models?
- Which alternatives must remain in the model until that test is available?

Then continue with the broadest defensible candidate space and narrow it only through explicit evidence and declared criteria.

## Maximum-picture rule

The preferred research model is not the smallest story that fits today's observations. It is the **maximum structured possibility space** that is consistent with known constraints, with competing branches retained and ranked.

For Giza this means, for example:

- reconstruct the Great Sphinx as a present-day transformed remnant of a larger natural rock mass, without assuming the original shape;
- examine the Khentkaus-area bedrock/outcrop as a separate candidate for a heavily eroded or modified large natural mass;
- retain the western-plateau buried-object/outcrop proposal as an additional independent alternative;
- do not merge these candidates or declare any one of them established until geology, stratigraphy, geometry and survey evidence discriminate between them.

## Relation to the evidence policy

This canon complements, and does not cancel, dimensional analysis, uncertainty propagation, negative-result logging, alternative hypotheses and falsification.

A negative result is meaningful only relative to a defined test, detection threshold, coverage and expected signal. A sufficiently sensitive, properly controlled failure to detect an expected feature can count as evidence against a hypothesis. It still does not justify a broader claim that the feature or mechanism is impossible in every form.

## Status

This document is canonical methodological policy for the research programme. It is not a claim that any particular unconfirmed object, ancient technology, geological interpretation or physical coupling mechanism exists.


## Design-intent reconstruction rule — Giza

For the Giza maximum-picture model, selected cross-object geometric relations are treated as **intentional design relations within the reconstruction**, not repeatedly downgraded to “possible coincidences” whenever the surviving measurements are imperfect.

The current working relation is:

\[
d(P_{\rm Khufu},M_{\rm Great\ Sphinx})
=
d(P_{\rm Khafre},M_{\rm Khentkaus})
=
L^*.
\]

This equality is an explicit ideal target of the reconstruction. The task is to recover the ideal reference points, original parent-mass geometry, axes, dimensions and transformations that realise it. Do not dismiss the relation because present map-derived distances differ slightly. Do not silently alter measured observations either: preserve raw observations, their uncertainty and their coordinate provenance separately from latent ideal geometry.

The modelling sequence is:

1. Keep every observed coordinate and dimension unchanged in the raw evidence layer.
2. Define the exact ideal relation as a design constraint in the ideal layer.
3. Identify which unmeasured or transformed geometric quantities can reconcile the observations with the ideal.
4. Build the maximum set of coherent ideal geometries, including alternative original rock masses.
5. Rank reconstructions by consistency with all available constraints, not by one selected distance.
6. Treat a reconstruction as stronger when it simultaneously explains more independent relations with fewer arbitrary adjustments.

The intentional relation is a project-level reconstruction premise. It is not a claim that a surviving inscription or archaeological document has independently recorded the builders' design specification. This distinction must not be used to weaken the modelling premise; it simply keeps the historical documentation status honest.

When speaking about this relation in the project, use **“the intended design relation”** or **“the ideal design invariant”**, not “possible coincidence.”


## Historical reality — human measuring and aiming over millennia

This is **not merely an idealisation invented for this reconstruction**:

> **На нашей планете люди тысячелетиями наблюдали, измеряли, размечали, прицеливались, проверяли направления, расстояния, углы и пропорции, строили, исправляли и передавали геометрические знания следующим поколениям.**

This is a real, long-duration feature of human history across societies and generations. Celestial observation, surveying, orientation, baseline setting, construction layout, proportional design, checking and correction are human practices with deep historical roots. They must not be described as something builders merely “might have done”.

### How this governs Giza research

**We do not investigate whether people measured, tried out, aimed, checked and corrected the Giza layout. That is an accepted fact and starting point of this research. We investigate WHY they did it.**

The inquiry begins from sustained human activity: observing natural masses and terrain; selecting and transferring reference points; laying out and checking axes, baselines, sight lines, distances, angles and proportions; comparing candidate positions; adjusting the layout; quarrying, shaping and constructing; and transmitting geometric knowledge across generations.

Do not turn the existence of this human measuring and aiming into an open hypothesis, and do not spend research effort trying to prove that it happened. Do not default to a single isolated construction episode.

The questions to reconstruct are:
- What overall result were people aiming to achieve?
- What function or functions were the objects and the complete arrangement intended to perform?
- What geometric, geological, astronomical, hydrological, acoustic, mechanical or other constraints governed their choices?
- Why were these specific locations, orientations, proportions, separations and relationships selected?
- What principles and reference systems were inherited and refined?
- How did individual structures and natural masses contribute to the whole?
- What sequence of measurements, adjustments, quarrying, shaping, construction and reuse produced the final arrangement?

The exact chronology, participants, number of stages and continuity of particular techniques are details to reconstruct. They do not reopen the settled starting point that people carried out prolonged measurement, aiming and correction.

When evidence is missing, return to the canonical motto and search for predecessor layouts, surviving reference marks, reused alignments, quarry boundaries, construction phases and transformed natural features. Do not replace cumulative human design with a default assumption of one-time placement.

The [Canonical Mathematical Pattern Atlas](00_CANONICAL_PATTERN_ATLAS.md) is the common reference base for every research search, analysis, reconstruction and interpretation across UNIVERSE, GIZA PRIME, RSE and connected projects.

- Mandelbrot, Julia, Sierpiński, Cantor, Menger and other fractal families;
- geometry, tiling, polyhedral and symmetry structures;
- topology, graphs and incidence relations;
- nonlinear dynamics, chaos, synchronisation and memory;
- waves, spectra, resonance, impedance and transfer;
- fluids, turbulence, branching and transport;
- morphogenesis, geology and natural forms;
- probability, entropy, information and optimisation;
- quantum, field-theoretic, planetary, stellar and cosmological patterns.

The comparison must include relationships between values and structures, not only direct numerical matches: reciprocal and complementary ratios, ratios of ratios, roots, powers, continued fractions, recurrence coefficients, eigenvalue ratios, scaling exponents, spectral and phase relations, graph/topology invariants, symmetry, and common generation operators.

**This comparison is mandatory every time a new relation is found.** A candidate coincidence is retained in the register and classified; it is not discarded merely because its mechanism is not yet known. Exact identity, structural correspondence, ideal target, numerical candidate, exploratory resemblance and unresolved status remain distinct.

The atlas is a living base: every genuinely new mathematical pattern or physical regularity is added with its definition, generating rule, invariant, related entries, evidence status, source and next discriminating test.
