# 11 — Giza and Richat: geology-first relational validation

## Geological baseline
The Great Sphinx is carved from natural limestone of the Mokattam Formation. Giza has layered Eocene carbonate geology and a fractured carbonate aquifer. These are physical boundary/material conditions, not proof of a designed microphone, transmitter or electrical contact.

Richat is an approximately 40 km eroded geological dome with concentric ridges and a documented igneous/hydrothermal history, including intrusive rocks, ring dikes, carbonatite-related rocks, hydrothermal alteration and erosion. Recent work proposes a polyphase chronology; modelled ages must be distinguished from directly observed present geometry. Natural dome/igneous/hydrothermal/erosional geology is the baseline alternative.

## Relational measurements
For every candidate pair, record 3D geometry and uncertainty; lithology, density, porosity, saturation, elastic tensor, conductivity, permittivity, permeability and temperature; air/rock/water boundaries; modal frequency, damping, mode shape, transfer function, phase, coherence and impulse response; calibrated source/receiver; input, stored, dissipated and output energy; alternatives and falsifiers.

Compare H(omega)=Y(omega)/X(omega), not merely two frequency labels. A cross-site relation requires a specified propagation path, transfer operator, delay, attenuation, coherence and energy budget.

## Giza protocol
Reconstruct parent outcrop and present residual geometry separately. Model the three large pyramids as a project-defined excitation/comparator ensemble and test each pyramid-to-comparator path individually. The Great Sphinx and Khentkaus monument/complex are two project-selected comparison elements in the research 3:2 grouping; Khentkaus is not an archaeologically identified second sculpted sphinx. Do not insert unmeasured chambers, throats or conductors.

## Richat protocol
Reproduce mapped dome/ring geology with lithologic, structural, gravity, magnetic, electrical and hydrothermal observations. Concentricity alone does not demonstrate an engineered circuit.

## Dimensional test: 0.2939775 Hz / 4,370 km
The period at 0.2939775 Hz is about 3.40162 s. If 4,370 km is proposed as one wavelength, required phase speed is fL≈1.28468e6 m/s; if a half-wavelength, 2fL≈2.56936e6 m/s. At that frequency, a 343 m/s acoustic wave has wavelength ~1.167 km, a 6 km/s seismic wave ~20.41 km, and a vacuum EM wave ~1.020e9 m. This conditional check does not exclude every long-range field relation; it shows that frequency and distance alone do not establish a carrier. Medium, mode number, boundaries, measured phase and attenuation are required.

Dimensionless candidates include kL, stress/modulus, damping ratio, Reynolds/Peclet/Prandtl/Rayleigh/Biot numbers (where assumptions fit), EM skin-depth/L and impedance ratios, network spectra, frequency ratios, coherence, phase delay and energy ratios with consistent SI units. A relation survives unit changes only if dimensionless and survives encoding changes only if invariant under a declared transformation group.

Protocol: build independent site baselines; calibrate sensors; repeat source/receiver placements; estimate transfer functions and uncertainty; compare with measured-property forward models; test natural-geology and no-coupling nulls; pre-register frequency bands and scales; validate on held-out data; publish negative results.

Established: same physical laws apply at both sites; Sphinx is carved in local limestone; Giza has layered carbonate geology and groundwater; Richat is a large eroded dome with igneous/hydrothermal interpretations. Unknown: any direct Giza–Richat EM/acoustic/hydrological/mechanical coupling, a measured carrier at 0.2939775 Hz, or a designed Sphinx function.
