# 00 — Scope, source baseline and evidence policy

## 1. Central objective

Reconstruct the open network of mutual relations and physical mechanisms of matter, fields, structures and processes across any spatial, temporal and energetic scales, determining how local and global properties emerge, transform, are inherited and become mutually consistent.

Operational rule:

**RATIO-FIRST · OPEN-SCALE · RELATIONSHIP-DRIVEN · EVIDENCE-GATED**

Absolute measurements remain necessary source data, but the research output is the relation, its uncertainty, mechanism and validity range.

## 3. Evidence classes

| Label | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| EXACT_IDENTITY | Follows exactly from algebra or definitions | 2^7=128, 42 XOR 85=127 |
| COMBINATORIAL_THEOREM | Follows from a defined finite structure | Q7 face vector |
| CODE_FACT | Directly present in pinned source | N=128, GATE_42=42 |
| REPRODUCED_NUMERIC | Recalculated from source equations | PI_128, reset steps |
| ESTABLISHED_MATH | Standard mathematical structure, independent of v2 | dim so(8)=28 |
| CROSS_STRUCTURE_CORRESPONDENCE | Independent definitions yield the same number or ratio | 42=10(2^2)+2=2*C(7,2) |
| MODEL_HYPOTHESIS | Proposed interpretation to test | gates as physical routing states |
| PHYSICAL_HYPOTHESIS | Proposed relation to measured natural systems | Giza/Rishat complementary EM behaviour |
| UNKNOWN | Not yet derived or measured | physical role of the Giza 3:2 grouping |

Do not promote a correspondence to a physical claim merely because the arithmetic is exact.

## 4. Relation passport

Every proposed relation should record:
1. RELATION_ID;
2. participants and their definitions;
3. mathematical variables and units;
4. normalization rule;
5. exact derivation or source;
6. uncertainty and sensitivity;
7. domain of validity;
8. mechanism, if known;
9. alternatives;
10. falsifier;
11. evidence class and review status.

A ratio between quantities with different dimensions is inadmissible unless a characteristic scale or dimensionally valid group is supplied.

## 5. Selection effects and multiple comparisons

The model contains many integers, exponents, constants, labels and visual parameters. Searching all possible pairings after seeing values creates a multiple-comparisons problem.

Therefore:
- keep exact identities, but do not describe them as independent confirmations;
- distinguish relations present in the original source from relations discovered later;
- preserve failed matches and negative results;
- do not change exponents after comparing with physical data unless a new derivation is made independently;
- define candidate relations and tolerances before inspecting holdout data;
- compare against shuffled, randomized and structurally matched null models.

## 6. Semantic boundaries

- Legacy GATE_42 is an integer node/index. It is not automatically identical to the later project-level observer-origin meaning assigned to 42.
- The source label H3 does not mean H3 group operations are implemented.
- The source label SO(8) does not mean the SO(8) Lie algebra or triality is implemented.
- PI_128 is a finite approximation to mathematical pi.
- A model quantity called energy is not automatically physical energy in joules.
- A 3:2 count of selected Giza elements does not establish a functional 3:2 mechanism.
- The same laws of electromagnetism apply at Rishat and Giza; that fact alone does not establish a direct coupling between the sites.

## 7. Open-scale rule

No spatial, temporal or energetic scale is excluded in advance. This does not assert that all scales are connected or that one invariant must persist across them. A relation may be invariant, scale-dependent, regime-dependent, emergent, absent or not yet measurable.


## 8. Canonical motto — absence of evidence

> **Отсутствие доказательства не является доказательством отсутствия.**

This is a governing research principle, not permission to treat every unsupported possibility as true. Non-detection leaves a hypothesis unresolved unless the test had adequate coverage, sensitivity, preservation conditions and power to detect the predicted feature. Lack of disproof is not proof of existence.

When the next step is unclear, explicitly inspect missing evidence, preservation, erosion, burial, later modification, access, sampling, instrument sensitivity, measurement resolution, noise and method suitability. Build and retain the maximum defensible set of candidate models, including idealized reconstructions for missing measurements. Label each value as measured, source-reported, reconstructed, idealized, inferred, assumed or unknown. Then design an independent test and state its discriminating outcomes and falsifiers.

The full governing policy is in [00_CANONICAL_RESEARCH_PRINCIPLES.md](00_CANONICAL_RESEARCH_PRINCIPLES.md).


## 9. Historical context — millennia of human measurement and aiming

Human societies have measured, surveyed, aligned, aimed, checked and refined the geometry of their built and natural environments over millennia. This is a real historical context, not a hypothetical modelling device.

For this programme, the fact that people repeatedly measured, tried out, aimed, checked and corrected the Giza arrangement over a long period is a fixed starting point, not a hypothesis to be proven. **The research question is WHY they did it:** what overall result and function they sought, what constraints and invariants governed their choices, why they selected these positions and proportions, and how the objects worked together. Reconstruct chronology and technical methods as supporting details, not as a reason to reopen whether prolonged human aiming occurred.

See [00_CANONICAL_RESEARCH_PRINCIPLES.md](00_CANONICAL_RESEARCH_PRINCIPLES.md).
